Know the Threats

Cyber threats are harmful activities that can affect computers, networks and personal information. Understanding these threats can help us stay safe online.

Phishing

Phishing is a cyber attack where attackers use fake emails, messages or websites to trick users into sharing sensitive information.

How to Stay Safe

Check the sender, avoid suspicious links and never share passwords through unknown websites.

Malware

Malware is malicious software designed to damage a computer, steal information or gain unauthorized access.

How to Stay Safe

Install trusted software and keep your operating system and antivirus updated.

Password Attack

Attackers may try to guess or steal passwords to gain unauthorized access to online accounts.

How to Stay Safe

Use strong and unique passwords and enable two-factor authentication whenever possible.

Ransomware

Ransomware is malware that can encrypt files and prevent users from accessing their data.

How to Stay Safe

Keep regular backups and avoid downloading files from unknown sources.

Identity Theft

Identity theft happens when someone uses another person's personal information without permission.

How to Stay Safe

Protect personal information and avoid sharing sensitive details on suspicious websites.

DDoS Attack

A DDoS attack attempts to make a website or online service unavailable by sending a large amount of traffic to it.

How to Stay Safe

Organizations can use traffic monitoring and security services to detect unusual activity.

Stay Aware. Stay Secure.

Learning about cyber threats is the first step towards protecting yourself in the digital world.
