Protect Yourself Online

Cybersecurity is not only about using advanced security software. Simple and careful online habits can help protect your personal information, accounts and devices from cyber threats.

Use Strong Passwords

Create strong and unique passwords for every online account. A good password should contain uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers and special characters.

Avoid using your name, date of birth, phone number or simple words as passwords.

Use Two-Factor Authentication

Two-factor authentication adds another layer of security to your online accounts.

Even if someone knows your password, they may not be able to access your account without the second verification step.

Be Careful With Emails

Do not immediately trust emails asking for passwords, bank information or other personal details.

Check the sender's email address and look for suspicious links before clicking anything.

Browse Safely

Avoid visiting suspicious websites or downloading files from unknown sources.

Look for secure connections and make sure the website address is correct before entering sensitive information.

Secure Your Mobile

Use a screen lock, fingerprint or face recognition to protect your smartphone.

Install applications only from trusted sources and remove applications that you no longer use.

Keep Software Updated

Software updates often contain important security fixes and improvements.

Keep your operating system, browser and applications updated to reduce security risks.

Backup Important Data

Regular backups can help you recover important files if your device is damaged or affected by malware or ransomware.

Keep important backups in a safe location.

Be Careful on Public Wi-Fi

Public Wi-Fi networks may not always be secure. Avoid performing sensitive activities on unknown networks.

Be especially careful when accessing banking or other important accounts.

Daily Security Checklist

✓ Check Your Accounts

Look for unusual login attempts or activities that you do not recognize.

✓ Think Before You Click

Always check links, emails and attachments before opening them.

✓ Protect Your Personal Information

Do not share passwords, OTPs or sensitive information with unknown people.

✓ Lock Your Devices

Always lock your computer and smartphone when you are not using them.

Think Before You Click

Cybersecurity starts with awareness. By following simple security practices every day, you can significantly reduce the chances of becoming a victim of cyber attacks.
